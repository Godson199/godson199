### 🚀 About Me

I'm a **Physicist, Applied AI/ML Engineer, Data Scientist, and Data Analyst** with a background in **Physics/Electronics** and a strong interest in solving real-world problems through data and intelligent systems.

My work spans **data analytics, machine learning, LLM applications, and backend engineering**. I enjoy taking a problem from raw data and exploration to model development, API integration, and a working application.

**📊 Data Analytics**
I use data to understand problems, uncover patterns, and support better decisions. My experience includes **Excel, SQL, Python, Power BI, data cleaning, exploratory data analysis, statistical analysis, and data visualization**. I enjoy transforming raw datasets into clear insights that people can actually use.

**🧠 Data Science**
I work on the analytical and predictive side of data, including **feature engineering, model development, evaluation, and optimization**. I am interested in understanding not just whether a model works, but why it works, how reliable it is, and how it can be improved.

**🤖 AI/ML Engineering**
I build and explore **machine learning and LLM-powered applications**, including conversational AI, retrieval-augmented generation, semantic search, and intelligent systems. My focus is on connecting models to real applications through APIs, databases, and practical engineering.

**⚙️ Backend & Deployment**
I develop **production-grade APIs with FastAPI**, work with PostgreSQL and SQLAlchemy, and explore Docker and cloud deployment. I enjoy the engineering side of AI — making systems that are not only intelligent, but also usable, maintainable, and scalable.

**🔬 Why Physics Is a Huge Plus**
My Physics background gives me a strong foundation in **mathematics, mathematical modeling, problem-solving, and understanding complex systems**. Physics teaches you to break difficult problems into smaller parts, identify the variables that matter, test assumptions, and use evidence to explain what is happening.

That way of thinking is highly valuable in **data science and AI/ML**, where understanding the problem, choosing the right approach, and evaluating results are just as important as writing code.

It also allows me to approach AI from a **scientific and engineering perspective**, combining theoretical understanding with practical implementation. My interests in **Physics-Informed Machine Learning, scientific computing, and AI research** are a natural extension of this background.

I believe the combination of **Physics + Data + AI + Software Engineering** gives me a strong foundation for building intelligent systems and solving complex problems.
