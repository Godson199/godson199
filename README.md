### 🚀 About Me

I'm a **Physicist, Applied AI/ML Engineer, Data Scientist, and Data Analyst** who enjoys using technology to solve real-world problems. My journey has taken me from studying Physics and Electronics to working with data, building machine learning models, developing AI applications, and creating APIs that bring these solutions to life. I enjoy learning by building and figuring out how things work rather than just using them.

My work in **Data Analytics and Data Science** involves taking raw data and turning it into something useful. I work with **Excel, Power BI, SQL, Python, statistics, data visualization, and machine learning** to explore data, find patterns, answer questions, and support better decisions. What I enjoy most is going from a messy dataset to a clear insight or a model that can actually help solve a problem.

On the **AI/ML side**, I'm particularly interested in building practical AI systems rather than just experimenting with models. I've worked with **machine learning, deep learning, LLMs, RAG, embeddings, semantic search, and conversational AI**, while also exploring the engineering behind these systems using **FastAPI, PostgreSQL, Docker, and cloud technologies**. I enjoy connecting all these pieces to turn an idea into something people can actually use.

Being a **Physicist has been a huge advantage** in everything I do with data and AI. Physics trained me to think analytically, work with mathematics, understand complex systems, question assumptions, and solve problems from first principles. It taught me to look beyond just getting an answer and understand *why* something works. I bring that mindset into Data Science and AI/ML, combining **scientific thinking, data, and software engineering** to approach problems from different angles.
